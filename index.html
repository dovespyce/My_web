FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to things clients usually want to know before working together.

How long does it take to build a website?
It depends on the project scope. A simple landing page can be ready in 3–5 days. A full business website typically takes 1–2 weeks. Complex web applications may take 3–6 weeks. I'll give you a clear timeline before we start.
How much does it cost to build a website?
Pricing depends on what you need — the design complexity, number of pages, and features required. I offer flexible, competitive rates. Contact me with your project details and I'll provide a transparent quote with no hidden fees.
Will my website work on mobile phones?
Absolutely. Every website I build is 100% mobile-responsive by default. I follow a mobile-first design approach, so your site looks and works perfectly on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktops.
Do you offer post-launch support?
Yes! I provide post-launch support to fix any bugs or issues after delivery. I also offer ongoing maintenance packages for clients who need regular updates, content changes, and performance monitoring.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Definitely. Whether your current site looks outdated, loads slowly, or doesn't work on mobile, I can redesign and rebuild it into a modern, fast, and attractive web presence.
Do you build e-commerce websites?
Yes, I build fully functional e-commerce websites with product pages, shopping carts, payment gateway integration (including Paystack and Flutterwave), and order management systems.
How do we communicate during a project?
I'm available via WhatsApp, email, and phone. I provide regular progress updates throughout the project and make sure you're always in the loop. Clear communication is something I take seriously.
Will I own the website after it's built?
Yes, 100%. Once the project is completed and payment is finalized, you own the full website — including all source code, assets, and files. Everything is handed over to you cleanly.
